Ashford & Rye Barbers

Concept website for a premium men's barbershop

Ember & Olive

Concept website for a premium wood-fire restaurant

A warm, cinematic restaurant site designed to make a table feel worth booking. The brief was to put the food first and turn first-time visitors into reservations.

It includes a brand story, a tabbed menu with food photography, a gallery, a private dining section, guest testimonials, opening hours, and reservation and contact forms. The layout adapts cleanly from desktop to mobile.
